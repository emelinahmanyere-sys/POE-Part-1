About Superior Designs Garments

Superior Designs Garments is a family-run business specialising in custom embroidery, personalised garments, branded clothing, and school uniform embroidery. We are passionate about providing affordable, professional embroidery services that help businesses, schools, and individuals showcase their identity with high-quality embroidered products.

We use a reliable Butterfly computerized embroidery machine to produce durable, professional-quality embroidery that lasts longer than many printed designs. Every project is completed with care because we believe every stitch helps bring our customers' ideas to life.

Who We Serve

Superior Designs Garments provides embroidery services for a variety of customers, including:

  • Small businesses needing embroidered logos on uniforms and workwear.
  • Schools and parents requiring names embroidered onto uniforms, jerseys, blazers, bags, and boarding school clothing.
  • Individuals looking for personalised gifts, baby clothing, birthday garments, wedding items, and keepsakes.
  • Churches, sports teams, clubs, and organisations requiring branded clothing for events.
  • Customers celebrating weddings, anniversaries, graduations, Valentine's Day, and other special occasions.
